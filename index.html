About Me

I'm a Computer Science student and I've always been curious about what happens behind the screen: how a message travels across the world in a fraction of a second, and what keeps it safe along the way.

That curiosity is what drew me to networking. I enjoy figuring out how systems connect, solving problems when they don't, and turning what I learn in class into projects I can actually show. This portfolio is one of them, built from scratch with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
